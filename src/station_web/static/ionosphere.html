Doppler and phase

Doppler shift
fD = −(1/2π)·Σdφ/Σdt, pooled over the 1 Hz carrier-phase steps in each point: the one Doppler estimator on this site (the time-of-flight section of the Propagation page draws the same numbers). Relative to the station's GPSDO-disciplined sample clock, whose floor shows below. A SHARED channel shows one trace, the composite carrier.
Phase variability (60 s detrend)
Standard deviation of the 1 Hz carrier phase about a straight line fitted over each 60 s window. At HF, multipath and mode mixing raise it as much as irregularities do, and no calibrated HF threshold exists, so the chart draws none. (The GNSS scintillation index uses a 0.1 Hz high-pass on 50 Hz phase; this measures something else.)
S4 (uncorrected for noise)
Normalised standard deviation of the tick SNR, as intensity, over each 60 s window. No noise correction: at low SNR the receiver noise alone lifts S4.
Carrier phase
Unwrapped phase tracks the path length. Ramps mean Doppler drift; jumps mean mode changes.
DC carrier phase (single-station channels)
Channels that hear one station (WWV 20 and 25 MHz): bare carrier phase, independent of tone detection.

phase-rate dTEC (assumes no layer-height motion)

What the rate measures: A single-frequency carrier phase changes when the TEC along the path changes and when the reflecting layer moves up or down. This page converts the whole phase rate to a TEC rate, which assumes the layer height holds still. Read it as phase-rate dTEC, not as a TEC measurement.

TEC, with GNSS VTEC as the reference

HF slant TEC against GNSS vertical TEC
Points: slant TEC along each HF path from the multi-frequency 1/f² fit (L3_tec); L3_tec publishes no uncertainty in TECU, so the hover shows the fit's RMS residual in ms. Dashed white line: GNSS VTEC over the receiver (L3_gnss_vtec), the reference. The two measure different things, slant along an oblique path against vertical over the receiver, so read the reference for its shape and level, not for point-by-point agreement.

Arrival residual against 1/f²: live group-delay fit
The window's last hour, same-mode points only: the slope gives 1.344 × TEC. A noisy validation product: the residuals' spread (1 to 2 ms) rivals the dispersion between adjacent frequencies, and R² under 0.7 or RMS over 2 ms marks an unreliable fit. Negative TEC means a mode misassignment or multipath.
